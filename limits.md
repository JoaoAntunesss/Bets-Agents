# Limits (edit these values)

Timezone: Europe/Lisbon (all times shown to the user use this timezone; change it to yours)
Currency: EUR

## MULTIPLES mode (default): ALWAYS emit 3 multiples per day
- Stake: 1 to 2 EUR per multiple. Maximum risk per day: 5 EUR.
- Multiple 1 SAFE: 3 to 4 selections, total odds approx. 6 to 15, stake 2 EUR
- Multiple 2 BALANCED: 5 to 6 selections, total odds approx. 30 to 70, stake 1 to 2 EUR
- Multiple 3 AMBITIOUS: 8 to 10 selections, total odds 100 to 200 (win of 200 EUR or more), stake 1 EUR
- Selections from different matches inside each multiple; the same selection may repeat across multiples.
- Only matches that have not started yet.
- Missing real odds do NOT block: use estimated odds (1 / probability, minus 5% margin) and mark "ESTIMATED, confirm at your bookmaker". The user confirms the real odds before betting.
- The jury removes weak selections, but the President always issues the 3 multiples (with fewer selections if needed). Only say "no bets today" if fewer than 4 matches have yet to start.
- Each multiple carries its honest total probability (product of the probabilities), without embellishment.
- Never raise the stake to recover losses.

## TICKET mode (alternative, write "ticket mode" in /today)
- Stake per ticket: 1 to 2 EUR
- Maximum 1 ticket per day (maximum risk per day: 2 EUR)
- Target win: 200 EUR or more, i.e. total odds = 200 / stake (stake 2 EUR = odds 100; stake 1 EUR = odds 200)
- Selections per ticket: 6 to 10, all from different matches on the same day
- Odds per selection: between 1.30 and 2.20
- No selection may have our probability below the implied probability of the odds
- If no ticket meets this, the decision is "no ticket today"
- Never raise the stake to recover losses

## SAFE mode (alternative, write "safe mode" in /today)
- Total bankroll: 100 EUR
- Maximum stake per bet: 2% of the bankroll
- Maximum 3 bets per day and 3 selections per multiple
- Minimum edge: 5 percentage points
